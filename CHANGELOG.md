# Changelog

## 0.5.0 – first Marketplace preview
- Colors follow the album art of the song playing on Spotify or YouTube Music (Windows).
- Optional album-art background behind your code (`poster`, `blurred` or `sharp` style).
- Status-bar song display with an options menu, and a one-click **Cover / Colors** switch.
- Your original colors are backed up and restored when the music stops or the feature is turned off.
- Ignores YouTube ads (anything under `minTrackSeconds`, default 60 s), so the theme stays on your song.
- With several players open (e.g. Spotify and a browser), follows the one Windows treats as current, and doesn't flip between them.
